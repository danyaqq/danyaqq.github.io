FAQ

I bought Premium, but the categories are still locked

On the main screen, tap the gear and choose “Restore purchases”. Make sure you’re signed in to the App Store with the Apple ID you used to buy it. This is also how you move Premium to a new device.

Is Premium a subscription?

No. Alias Premium is a one-time purchase: it unlocks every paid category forever, with no recurring charges.

How do I get a refund?

Refunds are handled by Apple. Send a request at reportaproblem.apple.com.

How do I change the language?

In the app’s settings, tap “Language” to open the iOS settings for Alias, where you can pick English, Russian, German, French, Spanish, Japanese or Korean. The word set changes together with the interface.

Where are the rules?

Tap “Game rules” on the main screen.
